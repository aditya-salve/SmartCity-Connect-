About

SmartCity Connect helps residents report civic problems and helps city teams review, prioritise and resolve them. Every report gets a unique ID, a location on the map, an optional photo, and a visible status: Pending, In Progress or Resolved.

The public dashboard shows issue types, locations (approximate) and statuses. Names and contact details you provide are private and visible only to administrators.
